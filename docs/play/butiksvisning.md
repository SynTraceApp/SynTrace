# Butiksvisning i Google Play

Texter att klistra in i Play Console under Butiksnärvaro > Huvudbutiksvisning och Översättningar. Gränser: namn 30 tecken, kort beskrivning 80, lång beskrivning 4000.

Standardspråk: engelska (visas för alla som inte har svenska). Svenska läggs till som översättning.

Bilder: ikonen `app/icons/play-store-512.png` är gemensam. Grafikbilden `app/icons/play-grafikbild-1024x500.png` är den svenska; den engelska är `app/icons/play-grafikbild-1024x500-en.png`.

Skärmbilder (1800 × 3600, telefon) ligger i `docs/play/skarmbilder/sv/` och `docs/play/skarmbilder/en/`: sex bilder i mörkt läge med exempeldata: startsidan, ett färgtest, Dina resultat, Min profil, hubbdiagrammet "Du" och Så fungerar SynTrace. Ladda upp de svenska under svenska och de engelska under engelska. Play tillåter 2–8.

Surfplattebilder (1800 × 3200, 9:16) ligger i `docs/play/skarmbilder/surfplatta/sv/` och `en/`. Play kräver dem eftersom appen är tillgänglig för surfplattor. Samma sex bilder kan användas för både 7- och 10-tumsplattor.

## English (default)

**App name** (24/30): Explore your synesthesia

**Short description** (77/80): Explore how letters, digits and sounds may connect with colours and feelings.

**Full description** (1387/4000):

SynTrace is an app for anyone who wonders whether they have synesthesia, or who is simply curious about how the senses can be connected.

Synesthesia means that one experience, such as a letter, a digit, a sound or a day of the week, automatically brings another with it, like a colour, a taste or a place in space. With SynTrace you take short tests and see which patterns appear in your own answers.

How it works
• Start with a few questions about yourself.
• Choose among several tests: colours for letters and digits, sounds, time and space, words and taste and more. You pick which ones and in what order, and you can continue where you left off.
• After a few days you do a follow-up. How similar your answers are over time says more than what you answer on a single occasion.
• You get a profile that shows the likelihood and consistency of your answers.

Good to know
• SynTrace does not give a diagnosis. The results are preliminary patterns and become more reliable only once the follow-ups are done.
• Your answers are saved only on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share your answers, and you decide first.
• The app is a work in progress developed together with researchers. It is not a validated test instrument.
• The app is available in Swedish, English, French, German and Spanish.
